Laplace law and ventricular hypertrophy

Wall stress T = P × r / 2h. Change load, then let the wall adapt and watch stress return to normal.

T = P × r2h
Marker = normal stress

Pressure overload: P rises, so T rises. The wall thickens inward (concentric), cavity stays small or shrinks, and T falls back. Volume overload: r rises, so T rises. The cavity enlarges and the wall thickens in proportion (eccentric). Values are illustrative, not clinical.
